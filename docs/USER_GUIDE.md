# BRMS User Guide

## Administrator
Can access every module, maintain the building structure, tenants/assignments, rent, utilities, maintenance, reports, and manage authorized users.

## Building Manager
Can manage building operations, floors, shops, tenants/assignments, rent, utilities, maintenance and reports. User administration is hidden and blocked by the API.

## Accountant
Can view reference/operational data and manage rent and utility records. Financial reports are available. Structural and tenant-maintenance actions are read-only.

## Suggested faculty demonstration flow
1. Login as Administrator.
2. Show the Executive Dashboard and explain occupancy + monthly collection KPIs.
3. Open Building → Floors → Shops to demonstrate the hierarchy.
4. Open Tenants and assign an unassigned tenant to an available shop.
5. Record a monthly Rent payment and one Utility bill.
6. Add a Maintenance record.
7. Generate the Monthly Report and use Print/Export CSV.
8. Open User Access to demonstrate role-based authorization.
9. Logout and sign in as Accountant to show that management actions are restricted.
